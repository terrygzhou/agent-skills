# CCDV-F Logistics (re-verified 2026-10-13)

## Exam facts

- Fee $125 USD, before any partner-tier discount.
- 53 items, standalone MC + multiple-response, 120 min; pass 720/1000; 12-month validity.
- Counts toward Claude Partner Network tier eligibility.

## Access path

- Open only to people at **Claude Partner Network** organisations; register with a
  partner email on a recognized company domain (personal email fails; unknown domains
  need a partner-support@anthropic.com request, 7-10 days to take effect).
- Portal: `anthropic-partners.skilljar.com` (old `anthropic.skilljar.com` redirects).
  The CCDV-F page is a register-and-pay page; exam guide, prep courses, and the 3 PDFs
  (Exam Guide, Certification T&Cs, Anthropic Certification Exam Policy) are linked there.
- Individuals without a qualifying org: start the free prep courses on the portal
  immediately; broader public access was expected later in 2026 (no confirmed timeline).
- Registration is valid 5 years from purchase; no sitting deadline inside that window.

## Exam-day mechanics

- Government-issued ID, name must match registration exactly; mismatch = no entry + fee
  forfeited. Name corrections via certifications-support@anthropic.com, subject "Name
  Correction Request", before scheduling. Age 18+ verified at check-in.
- Reschedule/cancel at least 48h before the appointment = free (refund if cancelling);
  inside 48h or no-show = fee forfeited.
- Retakes: 14 / 30 / 90 days after failed attempts 1/2/3; max 4 attempts per rolling 12
  months; each attempt at the standard fee (partner discount applies equally).
- Recertification: on-time = free, non-proctored open-book assessment on the Partner
  Academy (extends validity 12 months from current expiry, retakeable as needed); lapsed =
  full resit at full fee.
- OnVUE exclusions: IDs from Belarus, Cuba, North Korea, Russia, Syria, or restricted
  regions of Ukraine must use a test centre; Iran has a separate OFAC-driven policy.
